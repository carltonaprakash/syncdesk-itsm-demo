New to IT support? Follow these ten steps in order. Each one takes a few minutes and teaches a real service desk habit. Tick each step when you finish it.

Key terms

TermMeaning
IncidentSomething is broken or not working for a user, such as VPN not connecting.
Service requestA user asks for something new, such as a laptop or access to a folder.
ProblemThe underlying cause behind one or more repeat incidents.
ChangeA planned update to a system. It needs approval before it is carried out.
Priority (P1 to P4)How urgent a ticket is. P1 is Critical and affects many people or a key service. P4 is Low.
SLAThe promised time to respond to and resolve a ticket.
L1 and L2L1 agents take the first call and fix common issues. L2 or developers handle deeper technical work.
EscalationPassing a ticket to a more specialist team when you cannot fix it yourself.
WorkaroundA temporary way to keep working until the real fix is ready.
Resolved and ClosedResolved means a fix was applied. Closed means the user confirmed it, or the ticket was finished.

Good habits for freshers

  • Check the knowledge base before you start troubleshooting.
  • Ask what changed, when it started, who is affected and what the exact error says.
  • Set priority by impact (how many people) and urgency (how soon it matters).
  • Write clear notes so the next person can continue without asking again.
  • Keep the user updated, and escalate early when you are stuck instead of waiting for the SLA to run out.
